    Introduction

    This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the MakeThemCall web application, website, services, and associated tools.

    Information We Collect

    If you sign in using your Google account, we may collect your:

    • Email address
    • Profile name and picture
    • Gmail settings, only if you grant explicit permission

    When you communicate with us or consent to receive SMS messages, we may also collect:

    • Your phone number
    • Records of your SMS consent
    • The date, time, and method by which consent was provided
    • Communications between you and MakeThemCall

    How We Use Your Data

    Your Gmail access is used solely to automate the creation and management of aliases under your account. We do not use Gmail access to send emails or access your inbox.

    Phone numbers and SMS-related information may be used to communicate with individuals who have agreed to receive text messages from us, including communications regarding website services, follow-ups, questions, scheduling, and related business communications.

    SMS Messaging and Consent

    We may send SMS messages to individuals who have provided consent to receive text messages from us.

    Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

    Phone numbers collected for SMS communication and SMS consent information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

    Individuals may withdraw their consent to receive SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to an SMS message from us.

    Data Storage and Security

    We use reasonable security measures designed to protect personal information.

    Google authentication tokens and temporary settings are handled using industry-standard security practices.

    Where necessary to provide our services or maintain compliance records, certain information, such as SMS consent records and communication information, may be retained for legitimate business, security, or compliance purposes.

    Third-Party Services

    This app uses Google OAuth for authentication. Google-related access is governed by Google's API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

    We may also use service providers necessary to operate our communications and other business services. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide those services.

    SMS consent information and phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

    Your Choices and Consent

    By using our services, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

    You may revoke access to your Google account at any time through your Google account permissions dashboard.

    You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

    Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date displayed at the top of this page will be updated when appropriate.

    Questions?
